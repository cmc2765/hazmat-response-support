Time Sequenced Size of Dangerous Radiation Zone and 0.01 R/Hour Boundary (Hot Zone) After a Hypothetical 10kT Nuclear Detonation at Ground Level

Depictions of the five planning zones: Dangerous Radiation Zone, Hot Zone, Severe Damage zone, Moderate Damage Zone, and Light Damage Zone. The depictions represent the extent of each of the five planning zones at three time points after a 10 kT detonation: 1-3 hours post-detonation, 3 hours post-detonation, and 48 hours post-detonation.


Source: Planning Guidance for Response to a Nuclear Detonation, 3rd ed. (PDF - 2.62 MB) (FEMA, 2022)

  • Note the footprint of the Damage Zones and the Radiation Boundaries
  • Radiation boundaries grow initially, but as radiation decays, boundaries contract over 48 hours.
  • Response managers will need to consider these areas to protect the public and responders.
